# Pickup Soccer — Match-Day Content

Generate clear, friendly, on-brand group communications. Pair with the **brand-design-kit** skill when the output is a graphic, email, or styled post (green/white). Pull stat figures using the **player-stats** skill for recaps.

## Voice

Warm, energetic, concise. Like a captain texting the group — welcoming to newcomers, never corporate. Light emoji is fine for chat/social (⚽️🟢) but keep it sparing. Always include the essentials up top; details below.

## Required Details (ask or fill from context)

Date, kickoff time, venue/field, format (e.g., 7v7), player cap, cost / who's covered, RSVP link, RSVP deadline.

## Templates

**1. Event invite / announcement**
> ⚽️ **[Day] Pickup — [Date]**
> 📍 [Field], [Area] · 🕒 [Time] · [Format], [cap] spots
> 💳 [Covered by dues / $X drop-in]
> RSVP by **[deadline]** 👉 [link]
> Bring a light & dark shirt. See you on the pitch! 🟢⚪️

**2. RSVP reminder (24–48h out)**
> ⏰ [N] spots left for **[Day]** at [Field]. We're at [X]/[cap]. Lock yours in before [deadline]: [link]

**3. Waitlist / spot-opened**
> 🔓 A spot just opened for **[Date]**! First to claim it is in: [link]

**4. Cancellation / weather**
> 🌧️ **[Date] is [cancelled / moved]** due to [reason]. [Refund/credit note]. Next session: [date]. Sorry for the short notice!

**5. Dues reminder**
> 💳 Friendly reminder: [month] dues ([amount]) are due. Paying keeps your RSVP active and the fields booked. Pay here: [link]. Already paid? Ignore this & thanks! 🙏

**6. Post-game recap**
> 📋 **[Date] Recap — [Score / "great turnout"]**
> ⚽️ Goals: [Player ×N, ...]
> 🅰️ Assists: [Player, ...]
> 🧤 Clean sheet: [GK]
> 🌟 Player of the Match: [Player]
> Season leaders: [Top scorer] ([G]), [Top assists] ([A]).
> Next up: [next date]. RSVP: [link]

## Rules

- Lead with the single most important line (date + RSVP, or the score).
- Keep chat/social posts under ~5 lines; put extras in a thread or below the fold.
- Always include a clear call to action (RSVP, pay, claim spot).
- Name players consistently (as stored in profiles); celebrate newcomers and reliability, not just top scorers.
- For styled/email versions, apply brand: white background, Flag Green `#008751` headers/buttons, charcoal text.
- Offer 2–3 length/tone variants when the channel isn't specified (text blast vs. email vs. graphic caption).
