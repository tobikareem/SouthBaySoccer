Getting started

Signing in

N9ja Bay is password-free. Sign in with the phone number attached to your Pickup Pal account, including the country code (for example +1 555 123 4567). If you do not have a Pickup Pal account yet, tap Sign up on Pickup Pal on the sign-in screen, create it, then come back and sign in.

"Enter a valid international phone number"

The number needs the country code and no spaces or dashes are required. If it still fails, the number may not match the one on your Pickup Pal account — open the Pickup Pal bot from the sign-in screen to check.

Games and RSVPs

RSVP vs check-in

An RSVP says you intend to play. Check-in, on game day, records that you are actually at the field. Both matter: capacity is based on RSVPs, but stats and attendance follow check-in.

The game is full

Tap Join waitlist. If someone cancels, the next eligible player on the waitlist is promoted automatically and moves into the going list.

I cannot RSVP

RSVP closes at the deadline shown on the session card. Before that, check that you have accepted the waiver — it is required before you can reserve a spot.

Game day

Check-in is not available

Check-in opens shortly before kick-off and closes shortly after. Outside that window a game admin can still check you in with a late override.

I am not on the roster

Use Not on the list? on the Game Day tab to claim your spot on a recent game.

Teams and stats

Picking teams

Game admins assign two to four captains, and captains draft from the checked-in roster. You can also use Auto-balance teams for an even split.

My stats are missing

Submitted stats show as pending until a captain or game admin confirms them. Only confirmed stats reach the season leaderboard.

The leaderboard looks wrong

Leaderboards are calculated from confirmed match records for the selected season and group. Pull down to refresh. If a result still looks wrong, email us with the date and venue.

Account

Profile tab → Sign out. This clears your session from the device.

Deleting your account

Changing your name or position

Your profile details come from Pickup Pal — use Edit on Pickup Pal on the Profile tab.
