Privacy Policy

N9ja Bay · Pickup soccer, organized.

Last updated: 8 August 2026

N9ja Bay helps a pickup soccer community schedule games, manage who is coming, run game-day check-in, and keep player stats. This policy explains what the app collects, why, and who it is shared with.

Information we collect

DataWhy
Phone numberUsed to sign you in through your Pickup Pal account. We store a one-way hash plus a masked form (for example •••• 4567) for display — not the number in plain text.
Display name and preferred positionShown on rosters, the player directory and leaderboards.
WhatsApp group membershipLinks you to the group chat your games are organized in. Stored as a one-way hash of the group identifier, never the message content. We read group membership; we never post to WhatsApp.
RSVPs, waitlist position and check-insTo run capacity, the waitlist and game-day attendance.
Match statisticsGoals, assists, ratings, likes and MVP awards, so the season leaderboard and your career stats work.
Emergency contact (optional)Held for safety at the field. Visible only to organizers, never in the public directory.
Waiver acceptanceA timestamped record that you accepted the waiver and code of conduct.
Sign-in tokensKept in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain) so you stay signed in. Removed when you sign out.

What we do not collect

  • No location tracking. The app never requests or records your device location.
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • No payment card details. Payments are not enabled in this version of the app.

How your information is used

Solely to operate the app: signing you in, showing sessions and rosters, enforcing capacity and waitlists, recording attendance, and calculating stats. We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling.

Who it is shared with

  • Pickup Pal — the account system that verifies your phone number and provides the games and group information the app reads.
  • Microsoft Azure — hosts the app's backend and database (United States).
  • Other players in your group — your display name, position, RSVP status and stats appear on rosters and leaderboards visible to your community.

We do not share your data with anyone else, except where required by law.

Diagnostics

We collect basic service diagnostics (request timing and error rates) to keep the app fast and reliable. These records are not used to identify you.

Retention and deletion

Account and match records are kept while your account is active so season stats stay accurate. You can request deletion of your account and personal data at any time by contacting us at the address below; we will action it within 30 days. Signing out immediately removes your session and cached data from the device.

Children

N9ja Bay is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If a parent or guardian believes we have, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using the details below.
